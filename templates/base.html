Smart Declaration System

Designed and built as a Computer Science & Engineering project demonstrating digital election nomination filing, dynamic Form 26 affidavits, and role-based statutory scrutiny.

Statutory Legal Framework

Simulating procedures under the Representation of the People Act, 1951, Conduct of Elections Rules, 1961, and Election Commission of India guidelines for Lok Sabha, Vidhan Sabha, and Local Body elections.

User Roles Simulated

  • 🔹 Candidate: Registration, Form 26, Asset declaration
  • 🔹 Returning Officer (RO): Statutory scrutiny & decisions
  • 🔹 Assistant RO (ARO): Verification & authorized duties
  • 🔹 System Admin: Officer assignment & election configuration
